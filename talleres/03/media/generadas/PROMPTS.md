## 1. Apertura

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: 16:9 opening visual for a premium high-school STEM workshop presentation
Primary request: Create a polished semi-realistic editorial 3D illustration of a diverse group of Chilean high-school students, around 16–17 years old, confidently developing an environmental technology prototype together.
Scene/backdrop: contemporary public-school innovation classroom in southern Chile, subtle rainy Osorno daylight through windows, clean worktable with a small smart recycling-bin model, microcontroller board, breadboard and a few sensors.
Subject: the students are the protagonists; they are discussing ideas, observing the prototype and collaborating with purpose. One adult facilitator is present only at the edge of the scene, listening rather than taking over.
Style/medium: premium editorial 3D illustration with realistic materials and expressive but natural people, sophisticated institutional presentation quality, clearly illustrative rather than documentary photography.
Composition/framing: cinematic wide 16:9 composition, strong focal triangle around the prototype, generous clean negative space in the upper-left for a slide title.
Lighting/mood: optimistic, intelligent, focused, warm human lighting balanced with cool daylight.
Color palette: deep navy, teal, green, clean white and restrained red accents; visually compatible with AIEP-style institutional materials without using any logo.
Constraints: credible classroom electronics, safe tidy workspace, age-appropriate clothing, inclusive group, no brand marks.
Avoid: no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no sci-fi holograms, no humanoid robot, no exaggerated celebration, no unsafe exposed mains electricity.
```
